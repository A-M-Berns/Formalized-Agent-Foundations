# Paper errata — Oesterheld & Conitzer (2022), *Safe Pareto Improvements for Delegated Game Playing*

Defects in the committed source (`oesterheld-conitzer-2022-spi.txt`, the JAAMAS-equivalent
preprint) found while formalizing. Line numbers refer to the extraction. "Statement-level"
means the printed statement or definition is changed by the repair and the Lean carrier
discloses it; "proof-level" means only the printed argument is affected. Items were first
listed in `scoping.md` §5 and cross-checked by the codex review of 2026-09-04
(`codex-review-2026-09-04.md`); D6 and D9 were revised by that review.
